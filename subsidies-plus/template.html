Help

A subsidy that can no longer be done can be removed without a fine in the game console:

api.cmd.sendCommand(api.cmd.makeScriptingSendEventCmd("", "Subvention", "_debugDespawn", { index = 1 }))
index = the subsidy's place in your list of accepted subsidies, from the top.

Subsidies+ is in beta and the numbers are still being tuned.
