    Towns and waste

      Cargo

      Any bulk or universal truck, wagon or ship carries them.

      Good to know

      • The game's "Supply Industry" subsidy never picks a landfill, the incinerator or a sorting plant as its target.
      • The mod changes saved games for good (new cargo, bins, landfills). Removing it from a save breaks the save.
      • Mod settings: "Town waste rules" sets how strictly towns demand bins. "Waste test mode" is only for testing: it adds test buildings, logs reports and pauses the game after six months.
